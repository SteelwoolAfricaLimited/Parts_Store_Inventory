Parts Store Inventory Tracker

Item Master · Purchases · Stock Balance · Requisitions

This dashboard computes Monthly Consumption, Consumption Duration, Re-order Level, Re-order Quantity and the Inventory Suggestion. Monthly Consumption = quantity issued ÷ number of months that item was actually requisitioned. When Expected Closing Stock falls below the Re-order Level, the item is flagged in the Re-order Alerts tab and an email is sent to the configured recipient.
